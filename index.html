About

I am a user researcher, product designer, and UX professional with many years of experience across research, product development, design, and leadership. My work sits at the intersection of understanding people, shipping the right thing, and helping organizations build a sustainable practice around both.

I use quantitative and qualitative research to ground designs in context: how people actually use products and services, what they need, and where friction hides. Design, for me, is not only interface polish or feature count — it is building things that bring value to people’s lives and that they are glad to recommend. I also invest in evangelism and collaboration: design thinking, bringing users into the process, and uniting research, design, engineering, and business around shared goals. In practice, that input makes better products and often makes the work itself clearer and easier to execute.

Research

Context for how people use products and services — mixed methods, journey understanding, and insight that teams can act on.

Design

Product and experience design focused on value, clarity, and craft — from strategy and IA through prototyping and UI.

Evangelise

Design thinking, cross-functional partnership, and making users part of the process so solutions hold up in the real world.

Selected accomplishments

  • Built and led research programs that combined interviews, testing, and analytics to inform product strategy, roadmaps, and design decisions.
  • Shipped product and service experiences in complex domains by aligning user needs with business constraints and technical reality.
  • Partnered with engineering, product, and marketing to establish shared vocabularies, workflows, and review practices that reduced rework and improved quality.
  • Advanced accessibility and inclusive design through audits, patterns, training, and collaboration with content and development teams (including WCAG-oriented work).
  • Delivered strategic artifacts — journey maps, opportunity assessments, and experience reviews — that leadership used to prioritize and fund the right work.
  • Mentored designers and researchers, raised research maturity in the organization, and helped teams adopt remote-friendly methods and tools.
